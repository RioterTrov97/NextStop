# UI redesign (November 2026)

A UI-only makeover: new colours, glass surfaces, line-coloured trip screens and a new logo. No feature, setting or
behaviour is removed, and the layouts recorded in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) keep their rows and columns.

## Decisions

- **Primary colour: eucalyptus.** `#3E6B5A` in light mode (white text, about 6:1), `#A8C5B5` in dark mode (dark text).
  Neutrals lean slightly sage. The scale is in `shared/brand.ts`. Red (NSW Government) was considered and rejected: red
  already means delays and cancellations, it is near T9 and light rail, and red and white suggests the app is official.
- **Font:** Inter, unchanged.
- **Glass like iOS 26**, on floating things only: the tab bar, the followed-trip mini card, sheets, dialogs, map buttons
  and sticky filter rows. Translucent fill, backdrop blur and saturation, a light top edge and a soft shadow. Never on
  the page header (iOS 27 blurs the status bar unless the top of the page is opaque), never on list cards, never glass
  on glass. Solid fallbacks: no `backdrop-filter` support, `prefers-contrast: more`, `prefers-reduced-transparency`, and
  the Settings switch "Reduce transparency" (iPhone Safari doesn't expose the iOS setting to web pages).
- **Line tints**, like Sangeet's per-song colours. The screen for a trip takes the colour of its line: trip details get a
  gradient from the line colour into the page (first line to last line when a trip changes mode), the followed-trip mini
  card takes the current ride's colour, Home gets a faint glow from the followed trip, station boards take the selected
  transport's colour, and search result cards get a faint wash of their line. Line badges keep the official colours
  unmodified; tints are derived from them for surfaces only. Grey and unknown lines fall back to eucalyptus. Settings has
  "Colour screens by line" to turn it off.
- **Header:** solid, the page colour (or the screen's tint), with dark or light text to match. The status bar strip and
  `theme-color` follow it.
- **Logo:** a location pin with a train front in it, circled by a tilted orbit that passes behind the pin at the top and
  in front at the bottom, with a bus and a light rail tram riding on the ring (solid silhouettes, no outlines). The app
  icon drops the vehicles; the small favicon is the pin and train. Generated by `npm run icons`.

## Feature checklist (must all survive)

- **Tabs:** Trips, History, Live, Settings, with labels; Trips returns to the last trips page.
- **Home:** Plan button; current trip card; weekend replacement-bus notice; saved trips with upcoming rows (time,
  arrives, line badges, platform and fare, occupancy with caption, countdown with status), edit, reorder (drag), remove
  (swipe and menu), save chip on previous trips, period grouping, empty state with logo.
- **Plan:** From and To search (stations, stops, addresses, Current location, Home, Work, recent), swap, mode picker,
  walking settings, Leave now / Depart at / Arrive by with time, Show trips.
- **Trip results:** back, refresh button (draining ring, spinner, tick), swap direction, save chip, mode picker, walking
  settings, hidden-trips notice, cards (station and platform, duration and fare, times, countdown or leave-in, badges,
  delay, alert, occupancy), earlier trips on pull-down or button, later trips.
- **Trip details sheet:** drag, flick to close, header countdown rule, follow / unfollow, replan banner, alerts, fare
  breakdown, rides ahead first and "Previous stops" folded, platforms, stops, walks, long-change warning, occupancy and
  carriage strip, live map (with full screen), save.
- **Live:** Alerts (My trips / Everything) and Departures (station search, recent stations); station board with refresh,
  transport tabs with counts, platform chips, departure cards, follow from board.
- **History:** Trips and Searches, swipe to remove, save chip.
- **Settings:** home and work places, theme (System / Light / Dark), default modes, walking to the first stop, walking
  at the end, walking pace, minimum change, Opal fare type, history length, backup export / import, clear actions with
  confirmation, About (data, maps, carriage loads credits, version).
- **Everywhere:** offline banner, error fallback, no-connection state, status bar dims under sheets and dialogs.
